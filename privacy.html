Privacy Policy

TV Jr. for iPhone · Updated November 20, 2026

TV Jr. doesn't collect, store, or share any information about you. There are no TV Jr. accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Your channels

The sources and channels you add (their names, links and favorites) are saved on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, TV Jr. also keeps a backup in your own iCloud storage so you can restore them on another iPhone. That copy is held by Apple under your Apple Account. The developer can't see it.

Streams, playlists and guides

When you watch a channel, your iPhone connects directly to the address you added to load the stream, playlist, channel logos and program guide. Those servers see your requests the way any website does, and their own privacy policies apply. TV Jr. doesn't provide any streams or route them through any server of its own.

HDHomeRun

To find and play an HDHomeRun tuner, TV Jr. talks to it on your local network. For "what's on now" listings, TV Jr. asks SiliconDust's guide service (api.hdhomerun.com) using the tuner's own device code, the same way SiliconDust's apps do. No personal information is sent. SiliconDust's privacy policy applies to that service.

Jellyfin

If you sign in to a Jellyfin server, TV Jr. talks only to that server. Your sign-in is kept in your iPhone's keychain, and your password isn't stored. What you watch and how far you got is saved to your own server so you can resume, the same as Jellyfin's own apps. Nothing passes through the developer.

HomeView

When you watch on HomeView, HomeView loads the same streams, either directly from where they come from or from your iPhone over your own network. Nothing passes through the developer.
